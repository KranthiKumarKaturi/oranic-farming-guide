About the project

How Smart Organic Farming Advisor works

A simple decision-support tool for preliminary crop suitability screening.

What information is checked?

  • Crop profile and its commonly suitable soil types.
  • Broad growing season information.
  • Water requirement compared with the farmer's water availability.
  • Farm location entered by the user, shown in the assessment for context.

What does the result mean?

The score is a transparent rule-based match, not an AI prediction, soil test or guarantee of crop yield. It does not currently fetch live weather or geospatial soil data.

What about uploaded photos?

Land/soil and crop photos can be attached to an assessment and viewed on the result page. Automatic image recognition is not implemented in this version.

How can a farmer improve the assessment?

Use a laboratory soil test for pH, electrical conductivity and nutrients; confirm local rainfall, irrigation and sowing dates; and consult the local agriculture department or extension officer.
